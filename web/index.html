A few useful answers

My reservation has expired. What next?

An expired reservation needs to be reviewed before payment. Keep its order reference and confirm availability before starting again.

My payment and order status disagree.

Retain the order and payment references. A payment receipt alone does not establish the latest settlement or fulfilment outcome; the account records need to be checked together.

How do I save a copy of my orders?

Use Export CSV in Order history. The export includes the current results and their recorded amounts, statuses and deadlines.

Does this preview charge my card?

No. This local product preview uses example accounts and a local payment service. No card, email or live collection is connected.
